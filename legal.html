6. The UIPrism prototype (separate service)

The prototype at designbridge-production.up.railway.app is not part of this website. It is currently in beta and has no user accounts.

Hosting. The prototype and its Storybook preview service run on Railway (Railway Corp., USA). Railway processes technical access data such as the IP address when you use the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

AI analysis of your input. If you upload a screenshot, the image is processed on the server and sent to the Gemini API of Google for analysis. If you import a URL or a repository, the server fetches that address, and extracts of its content are sent to the same service. This happens to provide the function you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The prototype uses the free tier of the Gemini API. According to Google's terms for this tier, submitted content and results may be used by Google to improve its products and may be reviewed by humans. Therefore, do not upload confidential material or images that contain personal data. Data may be transferred to the USA.

Storage. Uploaded images are kept on the server only temporarily, at most 15 minutes, and then deleted. Analysis results are held in the server's memory for the hand-off to Figma and for troubleshooting (the most recent runs, without image data). They are not written to a database and are lost when the service restarts.

Your browser. The prototype stores your latest result and a flag that you have already imported something in your browser (localStorage), so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted. You can delete it in your browser settings.

Typeface. The prototype loads the typeface Inter from Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA). Your IP address is transmitted to Google when it loads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Figma plugin. If you use the UIPrism plugin, it runs inside Figma, and Figma's privacy policy applies to that part.
